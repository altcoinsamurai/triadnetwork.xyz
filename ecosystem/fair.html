The Problem

Private rounds run on fragments

A private round today is assembled from parts that do not talk to each other. Sourcing happens in group chats, diligence lives in scattered folders, terms move through email, the legal handoff sits with a third party, and governance after close is a promise rather than a mechanism. The founder absorbs the administrative load. The investor absorbs the opacity. Both pay for it in time and in trust.

The Platform

How FAIR works

  • Curated sourcing, every deal is brought onto the platform by a vetted curator who has checked the team, the terms and the diligence package
  • Private deal rooms, listing through to final close in one operating room, with investor sourcing and registered interest tracked in place
  • Legal handoff, structuring and documentation carried inside the same workflow rather than bolted on at the end
  • Onchain governance, capital protected in non-custodial multi-sigs with ownership NFTs and smart contract enforced voting rights after close
  • FairOS and MCP, founders connect their own agents to the operating system, turning deal room maintenance into a continuous workflow
"Deals vetted by reputable curators."· FAIR
Why It Matters

The curator is the trust layer

FAIR's design makes a structural bet that most platforms avoid: access is not open, it is vouched for. A curator does not simply refer a deal, they attach their name to the diligence behind it. That is a reputation instrument, and reputation is the only filter in private markets that compounds rather than decays.

It is the same thesis Triad has been operating on from the beginning. Distribution is the moat, and capital follows credibility. A curator seat on FAIR is that thesis expressed on the capital side of the business rather than the content side.

Triad Network's Role

Curator engagement

  • Deal sourcing, bringing rounds from the Triad network onto the platform as vetted listings
  • Diligence, checking team, terms and documentation before a deal reaches a FAIR deal room
  • Distribution, campaign execution across the Triad creator roster for the rounds we stand behind
  • Content and research, institutional-grade coverage of the deals and the category around them
  • Investor introductions, routing qualified allocators from the Triad network into live deal rooms
