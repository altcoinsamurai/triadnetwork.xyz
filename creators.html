The Process

From application to first campaign. Five steps. No noise.

Triad doesn't run a turnstile. Every step below is gated by partners. Most applications won't advance past Step 02. That's by design.

Step 01

Application

Seven-section application. Identity, craft, audience data, work samples, deal history, thesis, attestation. Read by partners, not screeners, not interns.

~ 25 minutes · 10–14 day review
Step 02

Partner Interview

Advanced applications get a 45-minute call with a Triad partner. We test your thesis, your pipeline taste, and the audience numbers you submitted. Most applicants do not advance.

~ 45 min · By invitation
Step 03

Onboarding & Brand Buildout

Accepted creators sign a non-exclusive deal memo and receive full brand buildout in 48 hours. Custom PFP, banner, palette, bio, content templates. Assets are yours to keep regardless of cohort tenure.

~ 48 hours · For selected creators
Step 04

Roster Listing & Pipeline Access

You're placed in the Triad directory. The 550+ project pipeline opens. Briefs are matched to your niche, audience composition, and rates. You decide what to take.

~ Day 5 · Listed publicly
Step 05

First Campaign

Typically within 30 days of onboarding. Contract, brief refinement, payout, all handled. 70% to you within 7 days of delivery. Triad earns 30% on every sourced campaign.

~ Within 30 days · 70/30 split
