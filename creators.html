The Process

From application to first campaign. Five steps. No noise.

Triad doesn't run a turnstile. Every step below is gated by partners. Most applications won't advance past Step 02 — that's by design.

Step 01

Application

Seven-section application. Identity, craft, audience data, work samples, deal history, thesis, attestation. Read by partners — not screeners, not interns.

~ 25 minutes · 10–14 day review
Step 02

Partner Interview

Advanced applications get a 45-minute call with a Triad partner. We test your thesis, your pipeline taste, and the audience numbers you submitted. Most applicants do not advance.

~ 45 min · By invitation
Step 03

Onboarding & Brand Buildout

Accepted creators sign a non-exclusive deal memo and receive full brand buildout in 48 hours. Custom PFP, banner, palette, bio, content templates. Assets are yours to keep regardless of cohort tenure.

~ 48 hours · For selected creators
Step 04

Roster Listing & Pipeline Access

You're placed in the Triad directory. The 550+ project pipeline opens. Briefs are matched to your niche, audience composition, and rates. You decide what to take.

~ Day 5 · Listed publicly
Step 05

First Campaign

Typically within 30 days of onboarding. Contract, brief refinement, payout — all handled. 70% to you within 7 days of delivery. Triad earns 30% on every sourced campaign.

~ Within 30 days · 70/30 split

FAQ

Common questions.

How selective is the cohort?

Founding cohort is capped at 100 creators. Applications go through a 10–14 day partner review, and advanced applicants sit a 45-minute interview before acceptance. We'd rather under-recruit than over-promise. Most submissions don't advance — that's by design.

What does the cost structure look like?

Nothing upfront. Triad earns 30% on every campaign we source, paid out from gross campaign revenue. You keep 70%, paid within 7 days of delivery. No retainers, no signup fees, no equity. We earn when you earn — and we are deeply selective about who we earn with.

Is there a follower minimum?

No. The bar is thesis, taste, and consistency — not vanity counts. We will take a 1.2K-follower writer with a sharp point of view over a 50K account that posts memes for a living. The application is built to surface signal, not size.

What happens if my application is rejected?

You'll receive a response within 14 days with a short, specific reason. Reapplications are welcome one quarter after a rejection — we'd rather see you grow into the bar than try to talk our way around it.

Do I have to take every campaign you send?

No. You choose what to promote. We'll never pressure you to shill something off-thesis or off-brand. The no-go list you submit at application is treated as a hard filter — protecting your audience is how we both win long-term.

What does brand buildout include?

For accepted creators only: custom PFP, X banner, optimised bio with conversion CTA, content templates, complete colour palette, multi-platform adaptation. Delivered in 48 hours after onboarding. All assets are yours to keep permanently, even if you leave the network.

How do payouts work?

A project funds the campaign. You deliver the content. We verify delivery, then send your 70% to your preferred wallet or bank within 7 days. Triad keeps 30% for sourcing, contracts, brief refinement, and account management. Statements available on your dashboard.

Can I leave the network?

Anytime. No contracts, no lock-in, no exit fees. You keep every brand asset we built for you. The directory listing simply comes down.

Do you take equity in my brand?

Never. Your audience, your IP, your name. Triad only takes a campaign-level revenue split on the work we source. Nothing else, ever.
